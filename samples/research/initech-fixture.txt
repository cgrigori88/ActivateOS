Initech Financial Announces Multi-Year Infrastructure Modernization Program

DES MOINES — Initech Financial, a regional financial services firm, today
announced a three-year infrastructure modernization program aimed at reducing
datacenter operating costs by 30 percent and accelerating its move to a hybrid
cloud operating model. "Our virtualization estate has served us well, but
renewal economics have changed materially, and we are evaluating our options
across the stack," said the company's Chief Information Officer.

As part of the program, Initech said it will expand its platform engineering
organization, and the company's careers page currently lists eleven open
positions for automation engineers and site reliability engineers, including
roles focused on infrastructure-as-code and Kubernetes operations.

The company also confirmed that its enterprise agreement for its server
virtualization platform comes up for renewal in the second quarter of next
year, and that a cross-functional team is assessing both renewal and
migration scenarios. Separately, Initech completed the acquisition of a
smaller regional lender in June, and integration of the acquired company's
IT systems is expected to begin this fall.
