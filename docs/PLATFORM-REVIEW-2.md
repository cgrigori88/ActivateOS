# Nineteen Rooms, One Loop — full platform review (Aug 2026)

Context: the first unicorn roadmap (docs/UNICORN-ROADMAP.md) is largely
shipped — blind overlap, joint pursuit rooms, settlement ledger, routines,
BYO-bot MCP surface. This review answers two questions from a full
click-through of all twenty rooms: what to build next, and how to make the
platform's one loop — book in → overlap → decision → outreach → joint
pursuit → settled revenue → learning — visible and walkable.

## I. Feature bets beyond the current roadmap (by leverage)

1. **Guest seats — the growth engine.** A proposed partnership grants the
   counterpart's owner a FREE scoped tenant: redeem the invite, climb the
   ladder, sit in joint rooms, read settlement. Every co-sell becomes an
   acquisition event; no seat-priced incumbent can copy a free counterpart
   seat without breaking their pricing. Mostly built already (multi-tenancy,
   invites, RLS, consent fabric): guest = role-capped tenant + upgrade path.
2. **Closed-loop MDF.** MDF requests attach to joint campaigns; sends,
   engagement, sourced pipeline and settled revenue accrue automatically;
   the claim package assembles itself from the ledger both sides trust.
   "MDF that proves itself" — a named budget owner exists at every vendor.
3. **Partner scorecards.** Win rate on joint deals, cycle time,
   sourced/influenced mix, room responsiveness, category strengths — from
   settlement truth, not self-reporting. Surfaced wherever a partner is
   chosen. Only this platform sees both execution and settlement.
4. **Renewal co-sell radar.** Renewal-typed motions with their own playbook
   (engagement-decay risk, partner-attach from the scorecard), renewal lens
   on Pipeline. Detection/digests/Today actions already exist.
5. **Cloud co-sell rails.** Sync hyperscaler co-sell referrals in/out
   (ACE-style, marketplace private offers). Build behind a design partner
   who lives in those programs.
6. **Warm-intro requests (the EQL).** From any named-overlap account:
   request an intro → partner owner accepts → three-way email from their
   captured contact, on the ledger. The ecosystem-qualified lead as a
   consented first-class object.

## III. Three structural UX moves

A. **Partner Hub.** "Partner" is smeared across five rooms. One room per
   partner: their book, overlap ladder, grants in/out, joint pursuits,
   settlement statement, scorecard. Admin keeps governance; the Hub is
   where you WORK a partner.
B. **Nineteen → twelve, grouped by the loop.** Decide (Today, Review,
   Motions) · Build (Intake, Mapping, Campaigns, Contacts) · Partner
   (Partner Hub, Joint) · Measure (Pipeline, Goals, Analytics, Insights) ·
   Platform (Intelligence, Routines, Admin).
C. **Make the loop walkable.** ⌘K palette across all entities (biggest
   single navigation win); next-step CTAs at every terminal action
   (approve motion → "Compose the campaign"; commit import → "Watch it in
   Review"; accept pursuit → "Read the broker's play"); one word per
   concept in user copy ("list" never population, "partner" never lens).

## IV. Sequencing

| Phase | Ship | Why |
|---|---|---|
| B (now) | ⌘K · next-step CTAs · terminology pass · rail regroup + merges | days of work, every session improves |
| B+1 | Partner Hub (absorbs Joint) + partner scorecards v1 | structural fix and intelligence bet are one build |
| B+2 | Guest seats | growth loop, timed to the first design-partner pair |
| B+3 | Renewal radar · warm-intro requests | small; deepen the daily loop |
| Behind a partner | Closed-loop MDF · cloud co-sell rails | build against a real budget and referral pipeline |

**One line:** the moat is built — next is making the loop visible (twelve
rooms, one palette, next-step pulls), giving its central character a home
(Partner Hub), then letting the loop sell the product by itself (guest
seats). Everything else is the loop getting deeper where the money is.
