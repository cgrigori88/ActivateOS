# The Space Between Companies — PursuitOS unicorn roadmap (Aug 2026)

Context: xAI + Cursor launched Grok Bot (2026-08-11) — persistent cloud agents
with their own always-on computers, logins, memory, routines, and agent teams,
bundled at ~$120/mo tiers. Enterprise GTM operators (e.g. Krista Letz's
published SpaceX playbook) are already running personal "bot staffs": meeting
prep, inbox triage, prospecting research, forecast hygiene, decks.

## Thesis

**Grok Bot equips a person. PursuitOS equips a partnership.** A personal agent
lives inside one company's accounts and dies at the tenant boundary. Partner
revenue is made in the space *between* companies, and the only thing that works
there is governed trust. The trust fabric (partnerships, grants, surfacing,
audit, approval gates) is already built. The roadmap puts agents on it.

What Grok Bot commoditizes: individual productivity. What it structurally
cannot do: anything requiring the partner's book — neither side will hand a
partner's raw data to the other's personal bot. That blindness is a trust
boundary, and trust boundaries are where platforms get built.

## Act I — A pursuit staff inside every tenant (parity, but institutional)

1. **Account Expert rooms** — a standing agent per strategic account: watches
   evidence, engagement, renewal clock; weekly "what's genuinely new" rundown
   (state file — never repeat); flags buying signals into the queue.
   *Have already:* provider pipeline, evidence gating, engagement scores,
   account rooms. New: the synthesis agent + state.
2. **Routines engine** — operator-defined scheduled jobs in plain language on
   the Railway worker, drafts-only guardrails: morning brief pushed to the
   phone, follow-up drafts from replies, forecast-hygiene notes, MEDDPICC gap
   nudges. *Have already:* worker scheduler, inbound pipeline, approval gates.
3. **Company voice, not rep voice** — style profile from the tenant's approved
   sent outreach; every human edit of an AI draft becomes a proposed rule
   (institutionalized "anti-slop"). *Have already:* sent archive,
   operator_notes grounding, outcome learning. New: feedback→rule loop.
4. **Meeting prep & call intelligence** — calendar + call-notes connectors;
   pre-call brief from the account room; transcript → MEDDPICC updates,
   follow-up draft, feature requests as evidence. (Transcripts are just
   another provider.)

## Act II — Agents on the trust fabric (the moat)

5. **Joint pursuit rooms** — shared workspace per co-sell pursuit across two
   tenants; each side sees only surfaced fields; a *neutral broker agent* sees
   both granted slices and proposes sequencing, who-contacts-whom, deal-reg
   conflict resolution. No personal-agent platform can sit in this seat.
6. **Blind overlap** — overlap answered before disclosure: counts → quality
   bands → named accounts, each step a logged mutual grant. Endgame:
   cryptographic PSI. Inverts the cold-start problem of partner platforms.
7. **Agent-negotiated sharing** — each tenant's agent knows what its operator
   would share and wants back; agents negotiate grant shape; humans approve.
   Partnership setup collapses to one approval.
8. **Co-sell settlement ledger** — sourced/influenced attribution computed
   from the shared audit trail, identical on both sides; quarterly ROI
   statements neither side can dispute. Monetization: price on partnerships
   and settled revenue, not seats.
9. **Bring your own bot** — expose each tenant as a governed MCP surface so a
   rep's Grok Bot / Claude / Copilot queries *their own tenant* under their
   auth. Personal agents become clients, not competitors; enforcement lives
   under the API.

## Act III — The network (opt-in, aggregate-first, always)

10. **Partner discovery** — blind-overlap scores against opted-in tenants you
    haven't met ("3 companies overlap your book >20%"). Marketplace flywheel.
11. **Ecosystem benchmarks** — non-de-anonymizable aggregates: co-sell
    conversion by partner type/vertical, time-to-first-joint-deal.
12. **Playbook marketplace** — operators publish motion templates, routine
    recipes, joint-play patterns.

## Sequencing

| Phase | Ship | Proves |
|---|---|---|
| now → +2 mo | Routines, Account Expert rooms, morning brief, voice profile | daily-habit retention; "works while I sleep" |
| +2 → +5 mo | Joint pursuit rooms + blind overlap, settlement ledger v1 | a real two-tenant co-sell closing faster |
| +5 → +9 mo | BYO-bot MCP surface, calendar/notes connectors, negotiated grants | personal agents call us; partnership setup in a day |
| +9 mo → | Discovery, benchmarks, marketplace, PSI hardening | compounding network effects |

## What we don't build

- **No generic personal assistant.** Grok Bot/Claude/Copilot own generic
  productivity; we integrate (BYO-bot) and stay revenue-specific.
- **No silent cross-tenant learning.** Network products are opt-in and
  aggregate-only, forever. Trust is the moat.
- **No autonomy without approval gates.** Agents draft, propose, negotiate;
  humans approve motions, grants, sends. The gate is a feature, not friction.

**One line:** personal AI staff is becoming table stakes; every one of those
agents goes blind at the tenant wall, and partner-led revenue happens on the
other side of it. Own the governed space between companies — broker agents,
blind overlap, settlement ledger, partnership graph — and every personal agent
must call you.
