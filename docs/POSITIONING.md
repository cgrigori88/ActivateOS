# Positioning: the partner-context gap (task #83)

## The category, in one line

**Your CRM ends at your company's wall. Revenue doesn't.**

PursuitOS closes the partner-context gap: consented context, joint execution,
and settled revenue between companies.

## The landscape (vendor-agnostic frames)

Three adjacent categories, each stopping short of the same place:

1. **Account-mapping tools** stop at the map. They tell you which accounts you
   share, then everyone goes back to spreadsheets. Nothing runs the co-sell.
2. **Revenue-context platforms** stop at the wall. They fuse CRM, calls,
   email, and docs into one deal context — for ONE company's systems. The
   half of the deal that lives in the partner's tenant is invisible to them,
   and they cannot reach it, because cross-company context is not a connector
   problem: it is a trust problem.
3. **CRMs** record one side, after the fact.

The context that decides a co-sell deal — what the partner knows, who they
have, what they've committed, what actually settled — never lives in any of
those. It lives between companies. That is the partner-context gap, and only
a neutral, two-sided platform can close it.

## Why we win the gap (the proof points)

- **Consent fabric.** Blind-overlap ladder (counts → bands → named), every
  rung approved by both owners; the overlap can only contain accounts each
  side already has. Warm intros where ACCEPTING is the disclosure — the
  partner picks exactly one contact to reveal. Guest seats: an invite link
  mints the counterpart a free workspace, so the fabric grows itself.
- **Symmetric records.** Joint-room ledgers, broker proposals, revealed-intro
  snapshots — stored once, read identically by both tenants. Neither side's
  personal tooling can hold a record the other side must trust.
- **Settlement truth.** Sourced/influenced attribution computed from the
  jointly pursued accounts' outcomes, not self-reported. Partner scorecards
  (win rate, cycle, mix, responsiveness) derive from it.
- **Provenance discipline.** Every fused claim carries its source; sources
  earn trust from verification outcomes; contradictions are detected, not
  averaged away. Fused context WITHOUT per-claim provenance is the
  confident-garbage problem, accelerated.
- **The whole loop.** Context is not the product; the loop is: book in →
  overlap → decision → outreach → joint pursuit → settled revenue → learning.

## The context surfaces (shipped)

- **Deal Timeline** (account room): the flight recorder — evidence, sends,
  replies, motion lifecycle, opportunities, joint-room events, warm intros,
  renewal signals — one chronological record, every event with provenance,
  the partner half consent-filtered by construction.
- **`deal_context` (MCP)**: any AI the customer brings sees the WHOLE deal —
  both companies' consented halves plus divergence findings. A single-tenant
  context platform cannot serve this response.
- **Reality divergence** (Today): deterministic rules that name both records
  in conflict — stale deals, late-stage-but-silent, renewal uncovered, motion
  stalled, and the cross-company one no one else can run: the joint room is
  live but your pipeline is empty.

## The AI-interface shift (why the timing is now)

The market's biggest players now say the AI is the interface: frontier
assistants get direct, governed access to enterprise systems over open
protocols (MCP), and the CRM becomes something you talk to rather than
click through. Salesforce + Anthropic's Claudeforce (Aug 2026) is the
loudest statement of it — Claude calling CRM data, workflows, and
governance rules through headless MCP servers.

This shift is a tailwind, not a threat, and the positioning is
*behind* it, never against it:

- **Same protocol, day one.** PursuitOS already exposes its record over
  MCP (per-org keys, reads mirror the operator's screens, writes are
  drafts behind approval gates). Whatever assistant an enterprise
  standardizes on — the same one their CRM vendor just certified —
  plugs into PursuitOS the same way it plugs into the CRM.
- **The interface still needs something worth saying.** A conversational
  CRM can only repeat what someone typed into it. PursuitOS supplies
  what the CRM structurally lacks: verified external telemetry with
  provenance, the reconciliation between systems (does it tie out?),
  outcome learning, and the consented cross-company half. `deal_context`
  over MCP returns the whole deal — no single-tenant system can.
- **One line for the objection.** "Their announcement makes your CRM
  conversational. PursuitOS builds the verified, cross-company evidence
  the conversation should be grounded in — and hands it to the same AI
  over the same protocol."

What gets commoditized by the shift: thin AI layers over CRM fields
(summaries, field-drafted follow-ups). Ship nothing whose value is only
that.

## Objection handling

- *"Is this account mapping?"* — Mapping is our on-ramp, not our product.
  The map is rung one of a ladder that ends in settled revenue.
- *"Is this a revenue-context platform?"* — It is the two-sided one. Context
  that stops at your wall tells you what your systems think; a co-sell deal
  is decided by what BOTH sides know.
- *"We already have a CRM / revenue-intelligence stack."* — Keep it.
  PursuitOS holds what those structurally cannot: the consented record
  between you and each partner, and the settlement neither of you has to
  argue with.
